Why this exists

Everything here started as something we needed for our own work first. A farm that needed to track animals and crops. A municipal training program stuck on paper. A photography studio losing leads to slow paperwork. We built the fix, used it ourselves until it held up, then packaged it so you don't have to build your own.

That's the whole standard: if we wouldn't run it on our own operation, we don't sell it on yours.
